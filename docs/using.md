# Using Wishtory

## Refresh

1. **Open the history page in the game** and let the list load.
2. **Switch to Wishtory and press Refresh.**

That is the whole routine. The game only keeps about six months of history, so refresh now and then, and import an older export to keep the rest. If Wishtory cannot find the history page, open it in the game again and retry, or paste a history link by hand from the arrow next to **Refresh**, which can also download your whole history again.

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Ctrl` + `R` | Refresh |
| `Alt` + `1` / `2` / `3` | Open Genshin Impact / Star Rail / Zenless Zone Zero |
| `Ctrl` + `,` | Settings |
| `F1` | Help |

**History.** The History page lists your pulls, newest first. Choose how much of the list you see (*5★* only, *4★ +*, or *All pulls*), narrow it to one banner, or search by name; searching looks inside the pulls you chose to list, so pick *All pulls* to find a 3★. Each row shows the pity at which the pull happened as a number and a small bar. Above the table, the timeline places every top-rank pull along the days it happened on, so the gaps between them show at a glance; click a dot to search for that item. *Group by day* adds a heading for each day, click the Time heading to flip the order, and *Copy CSV* puts the pulls you are looking at on the clipboard.

**Lists.** The 5-star and 4-star lists on the Overview and the History table share two switches: *Icons + names*, *Names* or *Icons* for what each pull shows (the names-only and icons-only views fit the most on screen; hover an icon for its name), and *Newest first* or *Oldest first* for the order. Both choices are remembered for every game. The Overview shows the latest pulls of a banner and offers *Show all* for the rest.

**Live banners.** The banners running now sit at the top of the Overview on the artwork of what they feature, with the time left and your pity on them; click one to open its statistics below. Pictures are loaded from the hosts named in the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md) when they are needed, and kept by the app's web view the way a browser keeps them.

**Colors.** Settings has color themes; Graphite is the original look. A theme tints the surfaces and sets the accent, and the gold of 5-star and purple of 4-star never change.

Your history and catalog live in one folder: `%APPDATA%\app.wishtory.desktop` on Windows, `~/.local/share/app.wishtory.desktop` on Linux. Preferences are kept next to the app's other user data (see [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md)).

**Import and export.** Wishtory reads and writes the [UIGF](https://uigf.org) standard (v4.x, recommended for moving between trackers, plus older v2 and v3 for Genshin), SRGF v1.0 for Star Rail, and Paimon.moe backups for Genshin. Rows without a rarity are skipped, and the import summary tells you how many.

See also [Wishtory on Linux](linux.md) if a game is not found, and the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md).
