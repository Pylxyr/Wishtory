# Using Wishtory

## Refresh

1. **Open the history page in the game** and let the list load.
2. **Switch to Wishtory and press Refresh.**

That is the whole routine. The game only keeps about six months of history, so refresh now and then, and import an older export to keep the rest. If Wishtory cannot find the history page, open it in the game again and retry, or paste a history link by hand from the arrow next to **Refresh**.

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Ctrl` + `R` | Refresh |
| `Alt` + `1` / `2` / `3` | Open Genshin Impact / Star Rail / Zenless Zone Zero |
| `Ctrl` + `,` | Settings |
| `F1` | Help |

**Your first 5★.** The game keeps only about six months, so the oldest 5★ in your history may have been a 50/50 win or the guarantee after an older loss that is no longer listed. When that oldest 5★ was the featured item, the banner's detail asks which it was: *Won the 50/50*, *It was guaranteed* or *Not sure*. Your answer is remembered for that account and banner and counts in the result list and the win rate; *Not sure* leaves it as *Result unknown*, and *Change* asks again. A permanent-pool 5★ is a certain loss and is never asked about, and neither is a banner that tracks no 50/50.

**Lists.** The 5-star and 4-star lists on the Overview and the History timeline share two switches: *Icons + names*, *Names* or *Icons* for what each pull shows (the names-only and icons-only views fit the most on screen; hover an icon for its name), and *Newest first* or *Oldest first* for the order. In the full History table, click the Time heading to flip the order. Both choices are remembered for every game.

**Live banners.** The banners running now sit at the top of the Overview on the artwork of what they feature, with the time left and your pity on them; click one to open its statistics below. Artwork downloads once per picture and is kept with the item pictures, so *Clear item pictures* in Settings removes it too (it comes back when needed).

**Farming (Genshin).** In the panel on the left of the Genshin Overview, the talent books and weapon materials the domains offer today, with a picture of each, and who uses them. Switch between talent books and weapons at the top, and close the panel with the arrow in its corner to give the page the whole window; a thin strip stays so you can open it again, and Wishtory remembers your choice. The day is the game's farming day on your account's server (it starts at 04:00 server time), so it is right in every region. Pick another weekday to plan ahead; on Sundays every domain offers everything. The list is bundled with the app and refreshed with each release.

**Colors.** Settings has color themes; Graphite is the original look. A theme tints the surfaces and sets the accent, and the gold of 5-star and purple of 4-star never change.

Your history and catalog live in one folder: `%APPDATA%\app.wishtory.desktop` on Windows, `~/.local/share/app.wishtory.desktop` on Linux. Preferences and the picture cache are kept next to the app's other user data (see [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md)).

**Import and export.** Wishtory reads and writes the [UIGF](https://uigf.org) standard (v4.x, recommended for moving between trackers, plus older v2 and v3 for Genshin), SRGF v1.0 for Star Rail, and Paimon.moe backups for Genshin. Rows without a rarity are skipped, and the import summary tells you how many.

See also [Wishtory on Linux](linux.md) if a game is not found, and the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md).
